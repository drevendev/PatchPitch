Organize your workshop with new batch crafting

Idle Fantasy Shop is a cozy browser idle game about keeping a small shop in Latchford. We have introduced several practical changes to how you manage your resources and organize your workspace.

  • We added a Crafting Batch feature accessible through the Batch Board inside your Workshop. You can begin using this when you reach Workshop level 10, which allows for up to 3 ordered job entries. If you reach Workshop level 50, the Batch expands to hold up to 5 entries. Each entry requests between 1 and 99 completed crafting cycles rather than a specific amount of output items. While you may include duplicate job entries, the system executes the ordered list once and then stops. If an entry is missing ingredients, it will block that specific entry until supplied, and later entries cannot skip ahead. Workers may supply the necessary materials, though the Batch does not collect them automatically.
  • Advanced Gathering now functions with a shared biome pressure system where both you and your workers contribute pressure. This pressure is shared within each biome and slows down subsequent cycles there. Pressure recovers automatically when no pressure-causing gathering jobs are running in that biome; there is no manual action required to start recovery. Gathering brushwood, ashwood, and reed does not add pressure and does not block the recovery process. For any cycle already running, it keeps its originally quoted duration across the forest, marsh, fields, and riverbank biomes.
  • We have added 10 new items in total, consisting of 3 gathered raw materials and 7 craftable goods. The new raw materials are flax, beeswax, and slate. The seven new craftable goods include linen thread, linen, a slate marker, an oak peg rack, a waxed wrap, a linen market bag, and an oak counter tray. For those processing flax, the flow moves from flax to linen thread to linen. Additionally, purchasing Shelf Capacity V and VI sets each individual shelf to a total capacity of 60 and 80 units, respectively. These purchases require Trading level 40 and 60, the preceding capacity upgrade, and specific material and Gold costs. Reaching a Trading level does not grant these upgrades automatically.

Also in this update

  • Society milestone changes now include increased Locals purchase acceptance for the Commons milestone. The Civic Lantern Office milestone provides a stronger Illuminated Path for Lamplighters, and Stonevein offers an earlier construction discount. These effects apply to existing claimed milestone flags, so your previously claimed milestones also receive these added effects.
  • You will receive a notification upon your first acquisition of an Artisan Chest. The unopened chest counter now serves as a shortcut to view your Inventory Chests. In the Codex, the listing limit is now restricted to chest-source jobs you have already unlocked. Each listed source job displays the chance of yielding an Artisan Chest per completion. Opening a chest remains a manual choice for the player.
  • We have updated the interface with a shared Help guide beside the profession heading, which explains how to start jobs, repeat tasks, gain mastery, and perform shelf actions. Repeated help text has been removed from individual job cards. Society level details now expose all rewards, and new content labels and explanations are available in both English and Russian.
  • The Import and Reset actions replace your saved-game data rather than the save system itself. While the storage writes the replacement data, play remains paused. If the replacement remains unresolved, play stays paused and your current-game export remains available. If the storage confirms no replacement happened, you can continue your old game. For legacy recovery involving conflicting main-database and local-mirror saves from different playthroughs, all original copies can be downloaded before you choose which save to keep.

Note: We maintain your shop, workforce, purchases, progress, and random sequences during updates. As a precaution for your browser-saves, please export a backup from Settings before you clear your browser data or change devices.
